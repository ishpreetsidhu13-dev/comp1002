About Regency Radio Company

Pioneering the Future of Electronic Entertainment Since 1947

A Legacy of Innovation

Regency Radio Company is a firm that has pioneered radio technology almost 10 years since its establishment in 1947 by a team of insightful engineers and businessmen. Our mission was never complicated: we have tried to make the American home happy with the radio entertainment of the high quality.

Headquartered in Dallas, Texas, Regency has become a top-rated company in the country because of its consumer electronics with a small garage workshop. The three pillars (innovation, quality, and customer service) are what we have used to make our name your new favorite brand.

The invention of the TR-1 transistor radio is the achievement culmination of research and development over the years, mergers with a major technological corporation and our desire to bring top technological devices to the common user.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Dallas, Texas

Leadership Team

John W. Richardson

President & Chief Executive Officer

In 1947, Regency was established by John who believed home entertainment had to be transformed. He was a former Navy communications officer who saw the potential of the then upcoming semiconductor technology way ahead of others. Regency became more than 5 people under his management to a company of more than 500 employees.

Dr. Margaret K. Chen

Chief Technology Officer

Dr. Chen has an electronic engineering background of over 15 years working experience in electronic engineering which she brings with her to Regency. She was a graduate of MIT who received a PhD in Electrical Engineering and headed a research team which made our fundamental design of transistor circuits. The TR-1 has been relevant in the making through her innovations both economical and reliable.

Robert J. Williams

Vice President of Manufacturing

Robert manages the entire production business of the Regency and makes sure that each TR-1 is of the highest quality. His industrial engineering background and his 20 years of experience in the consumer electronics manufacture sector has made our manufacturing process flow very smooth to provide our customers with top-quality value.

Our Journey

1947

Company Founded

John W. Richardson establishes Regency Radio Company in a small Dallas garage with a team of three engineers.

1948

First Product Line

Launch of our initial series of home radio receivers, establishing Regency as a serious competitor in the electronics market.

1950

Manufacturing Expansion

Opening of our first dedicated manufacturing facility, allowing us to scale production and reduce costs for consumers.

1952

Partnership with Texas Instruments

Strategic alliance with Texas Instruments to develop next-generation semiconductor-based electronics.

1954

The TR-1 Revolution

Launch of the Regency TR-1, the world's first commercially available transistor radio, changing entertainment forever.

Our Partners

Regency's success is built on strong partnerships with industry leaders who share our commitment to innovation and quality.

Texas Instruments

Our primary partner in developing transistor technology. TI provides the high-quality germanium transistors that power the TR-1.

Collaborative partner in circuit design and engineering consultation, helping us optimize the TR-1's performance.

American Electronics Association

Active member supporting the growth of American electronics manufacturing and innovation.
